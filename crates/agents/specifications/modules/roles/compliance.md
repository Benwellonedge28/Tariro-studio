# Compliance reviewer role

**ZIP-AGENTS-ROLES-COMPLIANCE-001 · v1.1 · Owner:** Agents maintainers. **Maturity:** D4 candidate for the scope below.

**Planned source:** `crates/agents/src/roles/compliance.rs`

## Boundary and inputs

Compare the exact reviewed candidate and operation scope with the current approved obligations and appropriate verified evidence. Bind every determination to a policy revision, requirement ID, evidence reference and bounded reason. Keep delegated review and exception ownership explicit.

## Behavior and failure handling

A policy label alone supplies no compliance evidence. Mark an unresolved or absent mandatory observation as blocked. A valid exception must come from the owning authorized policy process, match exact applicability and remain live. Record an expired exception without treating it as coverage.

## Acceptance

**ZIP-AGENTS-ROLES-COMPLIANCE-T01.** Supply a named policy with no receipt, then an expired exception and an exact qualified observation. Observe blocked, blocked and verified for the individual requirement.
